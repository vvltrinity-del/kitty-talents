Getting Started

BASICS

Welcome, benevolent cat lover! Kitty Talents 3.0 turns wild felines into powerful, customizable companion RPG fighters. Your cats now feature deep progression trees, direct first- and third-person possession (Twining), deterministic resource breeding, and emergency tactical weapon switching!

🌿 Natural Spawns in Jungles & Villages

Wild Talented Cats now spawn naturally in Jungle biomes and around Villages (Plains, Savanna, Meadow, Forest, Taiga)! Keep an eye out for them lounging in trees or patrolling village paths.

Step 1: Taming & Training

Feed any vanilla wild cat or wild Talented Cat raw salmon, raw cod, or a Catnip Treat. Once tamed, the cat becomes a registered Talented Cat companion bound to your UUID!

Step 2: The Whistle & Work Clipboard

Craft a Kitty Whistle to command all nearby companions simultaneously (Stay, Follow, Roar/Hiss shockwave, Quiet mode, and Return Home). Use the Kitty Work Clipboard to assign cats to your production habitats without removing them from the physical world.

7-Tab Companion RPG Menu

INTERFACE

Right-clicking your cat with an empty hand (or sneaking) opens the completely overhauled Companion RPG Screen. The menu is compact and features an ambient gradient that keeps the live Minecraft world visible behind your panels!

🏠

1. Home Tab

View companion summary, hunger gauge, satiety, happiness, mode toggles (Docile, Follow, Stay, Wander, Tactical, Guard), and bed bindings.

⭐

2. Talents Tab

Spend earned talent points on active and passive perks. Reset anytime using the craftable Catnip Respec Elixir.

🛡️

3. Gear Tab

Equip Helmets, Chestplate Barding, Leggings, Paw Boots, Carried Weapons, Tools, and open the Kitty Pack backpack storage.

🎀

4. Style Tab

Customize dyed collars, collar tags, skin breeds (Panther, Snow Leopard, Nekomata), Hero Cape, and equip up to 3 simultaneous cosmetics.

📊

5. Stats Tab

Check raw combat attributes, damage multipliers, critical strike chance, dodge rating, elemental resistances, and production stats.

⚔️

6. Combat Tab

Select emergent roles (DPS, Tank, Healer, Bruiser, Mage, etc.), configure Primary/Secondary Elemental Affinities, and cycle active ability loadouts.

👁️

7. Twine Tab

Trigger Direct Twining Possession with one click. Review keyboard keybindings, mouse sensitivity, and innate feline capabilities.

Twining System — "I Am My Cat"

SIGNATURE MECHANIC

Inspired by Aniimo direct control, Twining seamlessly shifts your player camera into the cat's true eye level. You directly steer the cat's movement, jumps, dodge maneuvers, claws, abilities, and firearms.

🎯 HUD & Combat Indicators

While Twined, an unobtrusive RPG HUD displays in real-time: Live Cat HP, Feline Energy Bar (0-100), Ultimate Charge Meter, Active Ability Cards with real-time countdown timers in seconds, and active status effect badges!

Contoured Feline Armor

ARMADILLO-STYLE REDESIGN

Adapted directly from Armadillo Scute & Wolf Armor anatomy, Kitty Talents 3.0 armor fits feline contours like a glove. It features a snug skull cap, an angled back carapace, clean leg bracer cuffs, and an animated tail sleeve!

🪖

Snug Skull Cap (Helmet)

Form-fitting cap protecting crown, brow, and ear bases. Eyes, muzzle, and whiskers remain completely unobstructed with zero bulky goggles.

🛡️

Carapace Shell (Chestplate & Leggings)

Contoured armadillo-style shell draping over the spine and flanks. Slopes smoothly at a 45° angle when sitting down to form a solid back armor shield.

🐾

Leg Bracer Cuffs (Boots & Leggings)

Crisp horizontal bands wrapping around the mid-forelegs and hind hocks, providing defensive plate reinforcement with unrestricted stride.

🐈

Animated Tail Sleeve

Protective armor casing covering the tail segments while leaving the natural tip exposed. Fully animates during walks, sitting, and loafing.

🎨 Full Player Armor Material Support

Equip standard player armor: Leather (supports all 16 Minecraft dyes + RGB cauldren dyeing with authentic leather trim), Iron, Golden, Diamond, Netherite, Chainmail, and Turtle Shell!

Special Event: Neko Night

WORLD EVENT

🌕 The Moon Glows with Mystic Feline Energy

On rare nights under clear skies, the world triggers Neko Night! The skies shimmer with violet feline auroras. Hostile monsters gain +25% attack damage and movement speed, while wild cats awaken with mystic power.

✨

Rare Nekomata Spawns

There is a special chance for wild twin-tailed Nekomata cats to spawn in the wild during Neko Night, carrying natural spirit abilities.

💎

Tiered Wild Cats

Wild cats found during Neko Night have a rare chance to spawn already pre-tiered (Copper, Iron, or Gold production tiers)!

⚡

Empowered Companion Buffs

All tamed companions gain double Energy regeneration and enhanced critical strike chance while fighting under the Neko Night sky!

Mod Integrations & Compatibility

ECOSYSTEM
🔫

Timeless and Classics Zero (TaCZ)

Equip cats with firearms in their mouth slot! Cats track ammunition from their backpack inventory. If ammo runs out, they automatically swap to a sword from their inventory or unequip the firearm to use their claws!

🔮

Apotheosis & AttributesLib

Companion damage, health, and armor dynamically interact with Apotheosis attributes. Soft-fails cleanly when the mod is not installed.

🖥️

Dedicated Servers & LAN

100% server-authoritative code. All client rendering, models, and audio are strictly isolated—guaranteeing zero NoClassDefFoundError crashes on dedicated Linux/Windows servers.
