# Kitty Talents

**Trainable RPG cats for Minecraft 1.20.1 (Forge).** Tame a cat, raise it from a kitten, train its talents, give it gear and a job, and fight beside it. When you want to, become it with **Twine**.

Think *Doggy Talents*, but for cats, with a real combat system on top.

---

## What's in 4.0

- 🐾 **Living cats.** Kittens grow up through four stages. Every cat has a personality. Your bond deepens the more time you spend together.
- 🌳 **3D blocks and gear.** All ten cat blocks are new 3D models, plus a new **Cat Tree**. Capes sway, halos float and wings beat when the cat flies.
- ⚔️ **22 abilities.** Six new ones: Ice Lance, Blizzard, Toxic Cloud, Plague Bloom, Arcane Blink and Starfall.
- 🎒 **Drag-and-drop gear.** The Gear tab now works like a chest, with your inventory underneath.
- 🤝 **Playing together.** Co-owners get roles (Rider, Handler, Full). The whistle can skip co-owned cats and has a favourites row.
- 📦 **Data-driven.** Skins and 250+ balance numbers can be changed from a datapack, and addons can add talents, abilities, modes and jobs.

## Features

### 🎓 Progression
- Cats level up to **100** (configurable) and earn a skill point per level.
- **51 talents** across Combat, Defense, Mobility, Utility, Support and **Dire**. Mastering a talent unlocks a bonus perk.
- Catnip Treats level a cat directly. A **Dire Treat** awakens the five Dire talents: **Sky Feline flight**, **Mole Cat digging**, Frenzy Focus, Alchemy Kitty and Catnip Catastrophe.
- Changed your mind? The **Respec Elixir** refunds every rank you paid for.

### 🐈 Living cats
- **Growing up:** Newborn → Kitten → Junior → Adult. Feeding a kitten speeds it up.
- **Personalities:** Brave, Lazy, Curious, Playful, Shy or Greedy. Each has a bonus and a quirk you'll see in how the cat behaves, and kittens usually inherit a parent's trait.
- **Bond (levels 1 to 10):** grows from time together, petting, feeding, play and fighting side by side. Perks along the way end with **Soulmates** at level 10.

### ⚔️ Combat
- Every cat **stalks** unaware prey and **pounces** as its opening move. It also **dodges** arrows and creeper blasts and **retreats** when badly hurt.
- **12 behaviour modes:** Docile, Stay, Follow, Wander, Guard, Hunting, Aggressive, Tactical, Support, Fetch, Work and Return Home.
- **22 abilities:** 13 skills, 8 ultimates and a utility action. Your cat casts them on its own in a fight.
- Also: **12 roles**, **9 elements** with strengths and weaknesses, and status effects such as Burn, Freeze, Shock and Bleed.

### 🎮 Twine: become your cat
Press **TWINE** and you control the cat directly while your body sleeps safely where you left it.
- Claw, pounce, dodge, and cast your four ability slots (**V G B R**).
- **Team Synergy** (**X**) launches a combo attack with your other cats.
- Fly with Sky Feline, or shoot guns with TacZ installed.
- Your body is always restored, even if the cat dies, you log out or the game crashes.

### 🛠️ Jobs and production
- **9 jobs:** Farmer, Lumberjack, Miner, Rancher, Courier, Sorter, Fisher, Collector and Gunsmith (TacZ). A job only runs while the cat is clocked in.
- **10 production tiers**, from Standard to Netherite. Breed two cats of the same tier for a stronger kitten.
- A **Kitty Habitat** houses a cat that produces its tier's resource over time.

### 💞 Breeding
- **34 variants**, with a Variant Codex that shows every breeding recipe.
- Feed **Spirit Catnip** before breeding for a guaranteed two-tailed **Nekomata**.

### 🎀 Gear and style
- Weapon, four armor pieces, accessory, tool and three cosmetic slots.
- 3D accessories, including Angel Wings, Hero Cape, Crown, Halo and Witch Hat.
- **17 skins**, dyeable collars, and support for skin packs.

### 🏠 Care and furniture
- Food Bowl, Cat Bed, Scratching Post, Bathtub, **Cat Tree**, Feeding Station, Rice Mill, Treat Oven, Incubator, Work Station and Habitat.
- Cats get hungry. A cat at 0 HP is **downed, not dead**; revive it with a Golden Apple or Warm Milk.

### 📡 Tools
- **Kitty Whistle:** 31 commands for all your nearby cats.
- **Producer Stick:** call cats home from anywhere in the world.
- Also a Tracker, Locator Orb, Pet Carrier, Treat Bag and Laser Pointer.

## Compatibility
Built-in support for **TacZ** (cats use guns, plus a Gunsmith job), **Apotheosis**, **Curios**, **Jade** and **JEI**.
The optional **KittyTalentsCompat** addon adds Lootr, PlayerRevive and Sophisticated Backpacks support.

## Installing
- Minecraft **1.20.1**, Forge **47.x**, Java 17.
- Install on **both the server and every client**, using the **same build** on both.
- Worlds from Kitty Talents 1.0, 2.0 and 3.0 load as they are.

## For modpack makers
- Change any talent or ability number with a datapack. Run `/kittytalents tuning export` to see every default.
- Add skins with a datapack plus a resource pack.
- The addon API (2.0) can register talents, abilities, behaviour modes, jobs and AI goals.
