# Kitty Talents 5.1.0

> **Minecraft Forge 1.20.1** · New Habitat upgrade and Habitat supply fixes. Adds an item: update the server and every client together.

## ✨ New
- **Smelter Upgrade.** A new Kitty Habitat upgrade: put it in an upgrade slot and the raw ores your worker cat produces come out as ingots, as if they went through a furnace (raw iron becomes iron ingots, raw gold becomes gold ingots). It works with every ore a furnace can smelt, modded ores included, and needs no fuel. Diamonds, coal and other products stay as they are. One per Habitat is enough. Recipe: a Basic Productivity Upgrade in the middle, furnaces on the four sides, iron ingots in the corners. (Suggested by a player.)

## 🐛 Fixes
- **Habitat supplies now feed the worker.** The food slot feeds the worker whenever a whole meal fits (or it is below 50 hunger), whether the cat is housed inside or linked from outside. Before, only a housed cat ate from it, and only below 50 hunger, so food could sit in the slot for hours, and a linked worker could starve next to a full food slot. (Reported by a player.)
- **The catalyst slot only takes catalysts** (Resource and Catnip Catalysts, and whatever a Habitat recipe uses). It used to take anything, so shift-clicked items could end up in it and do nothing.
- Hovering an empty upgrade, food or catalyst slot now says what goes in it.
