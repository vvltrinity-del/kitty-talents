# Kitty Talents 4.0.1

> **Minecraft Forge 1.20.1** · Bug-fix release. Update the server and every client together.

A full combat test found these bugs. All are fixed, and the mod now has 69 more tests covering every ability, ultimate and Twine move.

## 🐾 Abilities
- **Ability kills now count.** Frost Nova, Ice Lance, Blizzard, Inferno Spin, Tidal Surge, Volt Dash, Tempest Claws, Shadow Strike, Soul Flame Barrage, Plague Bloom and Toxic Cloud gave no XP, kill count, bond, XP orbs or extra drops when they finished an enemy. They now do, like a claw kill. Their damage is unchanged.
- **Feline Dash** cost 35 Energy instead of 15.
- **Healing Purr** healed the cat twice.
- **Apex Predator** now strikes the four nearest enemies, not any four.

## 🎮 Twine
- **Team Synergy** no longer hurts, burns, slows or blinds co-owners, villagers or your other cats.
- **Pounce** no longer lands on your other cats.
- **Claw** hits the nearest enemy in front of you, instead of whatever was first in range (often something behind you). It never hits a friend.
- **Holding the claw button** swings twice a second. Before, weapon wear, knockback and Fire Aspect applied 20 times a second.
- **Perfect dodge** no longer drops a cat with more than 100 max Energy down to 100.
- **Dodging next to a cow or villager** no longer counts as a perfect dodge or slows them.

## ⚙️ Other
- Every hit involving a cat no longer throws and catches an internal error (a Neko Night check). Combat is a little lighter on the server.

## KittyTalentsCompat 4.0.1
- Built against Kitty Talents 4.0.1.
- Fixes the "Missing metadata in pack mod:kittytalentscompat" warning on startup.
