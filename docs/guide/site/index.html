Getting started

  1. Find a Talented Cat. They spawn in jungles and villages. Break grass, tall grass or ferns for Catnip Leaves (15% per block).
  2. Tame it. A Catnip Treat always works. Catnip Leaf works half the time, raw fish one time in three. A new cat starts with enough skill points to max out any one regular talent.
  3. Open its screen. Right-click your cat with a Stick or the Laser Pointer.
  4. Pick a mode and a talent. Follow is a good start. Spend your first points on the Talents tab.
  5. Feed it and give it a bed. Cats get hungry. A Food Bowl and a Cat Bed near home look after that.

Empty hand

Right-click: sit or stand. Sneak + right-click: pet (builds bond).

Stick or Laser Pointer

Opens the cat screen. It never makes you ride the cat.

Food in hand

Feeds the cat; heals it and fills hunger. Kittens grow faster.

Care

Hunger

Cats get hungry over time, slower while resting. Feed fish, cat food or treats by hand, or fill a Food Bowl (5 stacks) and they eat on their own.

Sleep and play

Cats sleep on a Cat Bed at night, scratch the Scratching Post and perch on a Cat Tree.

Downed, not dead

At 0 HP a cat is downed. Revive it with a Golden Apple, Milk or Warm Milk. Nine Lives can bring it back on its own, with a recharge. For real stakes, /gamerule kittyTalentsCatDeath true makes a downed cat die if it isn't revived within 60 seconds (change the time with /gamerule kittyTalentsBleedOutSeconds).

Healing

Healing Herbal Treat heals 14 HP. Feline Recovery Wrap heals 10 HP and cures Poison and Wither.

Living cats

Growing up

Kittens pass through four stages, growing in size: Newborn (42%) → Kitten (58%) → Junior (78%) → Adult (100%). Young cats can't be ridden. Feeding a kitten makes it grow faster, and treats more so.

Bond

Bond grows from time spent near you, petting, feeding, playing and kills made while you're close. It has 10 levels, shown as the bar on the Home tab. Perks unlock along the way:

Captain

At bond level 10 a cat can become your Captain (the ★ on its Home tab; one per owner, and it wears a crown). In Guard or Wander mode it makes monsters within 32 blocks glow. Perched on a Cat Tree, or leading a patrol, it rallies: your cats within 16 blocks deal +10% damage and working Habitats there run 5% faster.

Levels and talents

Experience

Cats earn XP from kills (theirs, including ability kills). Level n needs 20 + 10 × n XP. Each level gives 1 skill point. The cap is level 100 (server config).

Treats train levels

Catnip Treat 1–20 · Super 21–40 · Master 41–60 · Dire 61–100.

Rank cost

5-rank talents: ranks 1–2 cost 1 point, 3–5 cost 2 (8 total). 8-rank talents: 1, 1, 2, 2, 2, 3, 3, 3 (17 total). Dire ranks cost 3, 6, 9, 12, 15.

Mastery and respec

A talent at max rank unlocks its mastery perk. The Catnip Respec Elixir refunds every rank you paid for.

Dire talents (Sky Feline, Mole Cat, Frenzy Focus, Alchemy Kitty, Catnip Catastrophe) unlock after feeding the cat one Dire Treat.

Combat

Stalk and pounce

A cat creeps up crouched on an enemy that hasn't noticed it, then pounces as its opening move. Pounce Training adds reach and power.

Dodge and retreat

Cats dodge arrows and creeper blasts, and fall back to you when badly hurt (not in Aggressive mode).

Claw combo

Hits chain into a 4-step combo: normal, +15%, +30% with Bleed, then a +60% finisher with knockback. Hits 0.4 to 0.9 s apart are perfect: +25% damage and 10 Energy back.

Energy

Abilities, dodges and pounces cost Energy. Max Energy is 100, +2 per level (up to +100), plus talents and role. It refills 2/s in a fight, 4/s out of combat and 6/s while resting.

Perfect dodge

Dodging with an enemy close by refunds the Energy and slows and weakens the enemy.

Team Synergy

Your talents pick the combo: Inferno Hunt (a fire cat), Frozen Tide (water and snow cats), Twin Soul Assault (shadow or Nekomata), otherwise Pack Pounce Surge. 25 Energy, 5 s recharge. It never hits your friends.

Flight

With Sky Feline, hold jump to climb and glide.

Always safe

Your body comes back if the cat dies, you take heavy damage (below 25% health), log out, change dimension, or the game crashes.

Whistle and tools

The Kitty Whistle commands every cat of yours nearby at once. Right-click blows it; sneak + right-click picks the command; or open the whistle wheel from the Home tab. The wheel has a Mine only / Mine + co-owned switch, and right-clicking a command pins it to a favourites row.

  • Heel & Teleport Commands cats to immediately sprint or teleport to your side.
  • Stay (Docile) Commands cats to sit down and rest.
  • Follow Me Commands cats to follow and assist you.
  • Guard Area Commands cats to defend a 16-block territory.
  • Hunting Mode Commands cats to hunt small prey and pests.
  • Aggressive Commands cats to seek and attack hostile mobs.
  • Return Home Commands cats with linked beds to return home.
  • Stop Attacking Commands cats to immediately disengage from combat.
  • Ssshhh! (Quiet) Silences cats from meowing for 5 minutes.
  • Come Here Calls cats to your location.
  • All Stand Up Commands all sitting cats to stand up.
  • All Sit Down Commands all standing cats to sit down.
  • Attack Focus Directs cats to target your currently focused enemy.
  • Disengage & Calm Commands cats to break combat and calm down.
  • Defend Area Commands cats to guard the immediate area.
  • Patrol Perimeter Commands cats to patrol the perimeter.
  • Fetch Items Commands cats with Paw Retriever to fetch nearby items.
  • Scatter / Disperse Commands cats to disperse outward.
  • Rest on Bed Commands cats to rest on the nearest cat bed.
  • Eat from Bowl Commands cats to eat from the nearest food bowl.
  • Purr Buff Commands cats with Purr Therapy to provide healing & buffs.
  • Hiss / Intimidate Commands cats to hiss and scare creepers and foes.
  • Trigger Frenzy Boosts feline attack speed temporarily.
  • Locate Beacon Emits beacon particles to locate your cats.
  • Play with Toys Commands cats to play with nearby toys.
  • Fish in Water Commands cats with Fisherman talent to catch fish.
  • Mole Cat Dig Commands cats with Mole talent to dig underground.
  • Mount Takeoff Mount or takeoff if equipped with flight gear.
  • Open Cat Pack Opens the nearest cat's pack inventory.
  • Clock In (Work) Commands bound worker cats to clock in and resume their jobs.
  • Clock Out (Rest) Commands worker cats to clock out, return to station, and sit.

Lists your cats in every dimension and calls them to you, even from unloaded chunks. Sneak + right-click sends them to bed. Fireproof.

Tracker and Locator Orb

The Tracker scans 256 blocks. A Locator Orb bound to one cat shows where it is and how it's doing from anywhere.

Pet Carrier

Pick your cat up and carry it; right-click the ground to let it out.

Treat Bag

Stores 576 treats. Right-click a cat to feed one.

Jobs and production

  1. Place a Kitty Work Station near the work area. It holds what the cat gathers (the Collector drops its haul there).
  2. Right-click your cat with the Work Clipboard to select it, then use the clipboard on blocks to set the work area's corners (and link chests for Courier and Sorter).
  3. Pick the job on the Home tab. Picking a job clocks the cat in and puts it in Work mode. Clock it out to stop.

Production tiers and the Habitat

Every cat has a production tier. A tier treat raises it one step (Coal Cat Treat turns a Standard cat into a Coal cat, and so on). Higher tiers are tougher and hit harder, and two cats of the same tier breed a kitten of the next tier. A cat housed in a Kitty Habitat (use the clipboard on the Habitat) produces its tier's resource over time; Productivity Upgrades (up to 4 per Habitat) speed it up.

Personality and bond in the Habitat

A housed cat's trait changes how it works, and its neighbours matter, so plan the layout of your colony. The Habitat screen shows the total speed next to the cycle count; hover it for the list.

  • Shy: +20% with no other working Habitat within 4 blocks, -10% with 3 or more.
  • Brave (foreman): other working Habitats within 6 blocks get +5% (doesn't stack).
  • Lazy: 10% slower, but never gets hungry while working.
  • Curious: each cycle, a 10% chance of a bonus item from the tier below.
  • Playful: +15% with another Playful Habitat within 4 blocks, -10% with no working Habitat near.
  • Greedy: +25% output, +50% hunger.
  • Bond: +1% per bond level while you're within 32 blocks.

A housed Nekomata wards its Habitat with ghostly flames: hostile mobs within 6 blocks are pushed away. Out in the world, a Nekomata keeps up to 3 blue wisps circling it and sends them at its target (5 damage each; one regrows every 10 s).

Breeding

Kittens

Feed Breeding Catnip to two Talented Cats. The kitten is tamed, inherits a trait and a talent, and grows up over time.

34 variants

The Feline Variant Codex lists every variant, its breeding recipe and its production yield.

Nekomata

Feed Spirit Catnip to a parent before breeding for a guaranteed two-tailed Nekomata. Nekomata strike twice and get extra drops.

Gear and style

The Gear tab has a weapon slot (held in the mouth; a TacZ gun works too), helmet, chest, legs and boots, an accessory, a tool for work, and three cosmetic slots. Hover an empty slot to see what fits, and a red tooltip explains why an item doesn't (wrong slot, missing talent rank, or not your cat). Any cat can wear leather to iron armor and hold basic swords. Diamond gear needs Feline Armor or Cat Armory rank 1, netherite rank 3, and guns Cat Armory 1; each rank also adds real power.

On the Style tab: collar colour (dye works too), and 17 skins: All Black, Black, British Shorthair, Calico, Ghost Phantom, Golden Maneki, Jellie, Panther, Persian, Ragdoll, Red, Siamese, Snow Leopard, Tabby, Tiger, Void Ender, White. Servers can add more with a skin pack.

Playing together

Bases, guard posts and patrols

Craft a Base Pin (red dye over two iron nuggets). Right-click two corners to mark a base; right-click the air to rename, resize, merge or delete your bases. Sneak + right-click a cat, then sneak + right-click a block: that block is its guard post (on a Cat Tree it perches there for good). Guard cats in a base without a post patrol its border in teams of up to 3.

Add another online player as a co-owner from the Home tab, then click their name to set a role.

RoleCan
RiderRide the cat.
HandlerAlso command it (modes, whistle, jobs) and Twine with it.
FullAlso change its gear and style.

Only the owner can train, respec, rename or manage co-owners. A co-owner can leave on their own. Cats that aren't yours show their owner's name on the HUD, and other players see a label over a Twined cat and over the spot where its player's body rests.

Up to 4 cats can follow you at once (Follow, Tactical and Support); a fifth sits down instead. Change it with /gamerule kittyTalentsFollowerLimit (0 = no limit). The HUD shows small cards for your nearby cats that are following you, hurt or downed. Change it in config/kittytalents-client.toml (showFriendCats, showTwineMarkers, range).

Balance by datapack

Put JSON in data/<pack>/kittytalents/tuning/. Changes apply on /reload, and tooltips show the new numbers.

Skin packs

A skin is a JSON file in data/<pack>/kittytalents/skins/ plus a texture in a resource pack.

Server config

kittytalents-common.toml: max level, friendly fire, immortal cats, worker block breaking, worker chunk loading, Rancher culling, cat guns.

Compatibility

TacZ (guns, Gunsmith job), Apotheosis, Curios, Jade, JEI. The KittyTalentsCompat addon adds Lootr, PlayerRevive and Sophisticated Backpacks.

Questions

My cat won't follow me.

It's probably sitting (right-click with an empty hand) or in a mode that holds its spot. Switch it to Follow.

I can't Twine.

You can't Twine a downed cat, a cat someone else is Twined with, or while it's carrying a rider. You need to be close, and a co-owner needs the Handler role.

My cat isn't using its abilities.

It casts only in a fight, only in modes that fight, and only when it has the Energy. It saves each one for the right moment: heals when hurt, area attacks against a crowd. Hiss and Healing Purr from the default loadout often go first.

Things act strangely in multiplayer (cats untwine, the bond bar is missing).

The server and every player must run the same build of the mod.

I lost a cat.

Use the Producer Stick to call it, or a Locator Orb or Tracker to find it.
