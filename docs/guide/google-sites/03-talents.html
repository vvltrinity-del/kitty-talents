Levels and talents

Experience

Cats earn XP from kills (theirs, including ability kills). Level n needs 20 + 10 × n XP. Each level gives 1 skill point. The cap is level 100 (server config).

Treats train levels

Catnip Treat 1–20 · Super 21–40 · Master 41–60 · Dire 61–100.

Rank cost

5-rank talents: ranks 1–2 cost 1 point, 3–5 cost 2 (8 total). 8-rank talents: 1, 1, 2, 2, 2, 3, 3, 3 (17 total). Dire ranks cost 3, 6, 9, 12, 15.

Mastery and respec

A talent at max rank unlocks its mastery perk. The Catnip Respec Elixir refunds every rank you paid for.

Dire talents (Sky Feline, Mole Cat, Frenzy Focus, Alchemy Kitty, Catnip Catastrophe) unlock after feeding the cat one Dire Treat.
