Jobs and production

  1. Place a Kitty Work Station near the work area. It holds what the cat gathers (the Collector drops its haul there).
  2. Right-click your cat with the Work Clipboard to select it, then use the clipboard on blocks to set the work area's corners (and link chests for Courier and Sorter).
  3. Pick the job on the Home tab. Picking a job clocks the cat in and puts it in Work mode. Clock it out to stop.

Production tiers and the Habitat

Every cat has a production tier. A tier treat raises it one step (Coal Cat Treat turns a Standard cat into a Coal cat, and so on). Higher tiers are tougher and hit harder, and two cats of the same tier breed a kitten of the next tier. A cat housed in a Kitty Habitat (use the clipboard on the Habitat) produces its tier's resource over time; Productivity Upgrades (up to 4 per Habitat) speed it up.
