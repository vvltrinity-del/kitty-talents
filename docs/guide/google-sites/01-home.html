Getting started

  1. Find a Talented Cat. They spawn in jungles and villages. Break grass, tall grass or ferns for Catnip Leaves (15% per block).
  2. Tame it. A Catnip Treat always works. Catnip Leaf works half the time, raw fish one time in three. A new cat starts with enough skill points to max out any one regular talent.
  3. Open its screen. Right-click your cat with a Stick or the Laser Pointer.
  4. Pick a mode and a talent. Follow is a good start. Spend your first points on the Talents tab.
  5. Feed it and give it a bed. Cats get hungry. A Food Bowl and a Cat Bed near home look after that.

Empty hand

Right-click: sit or stand. Sneak + right-click: pet (builds bond).

Stick or Laser Pointer

Opens the cat screen. It never makes you ride the cat.

Food in hand

Feeds the cat; heals it and fills hunger. Kittens grow faster.

Care

Hunger

Cats get hungry over time, slower while resting. Feed fish, cat food or treats by hand, or fill a Food Bowl (5 stacks) and they eat on their own.

Sleep and play

Cats sleep on a Cat Bed at night, scratch the Scratching Post and perch on a Cat Tree.

Downed, not dead

At 0 HP a cat is downed. Revive it with a Golden Apple, Milk or Warm Milk. Nine Lives can bring it back on its own, with a recharge. For real stakes, /gamerule kittyTalentsCatDeath true makes a downed cat die if it isn't revived within 60 seconds (change the time with /gamerule kittyTalentsBleedOutSeconds).

Healing

Healing Herbal Treat heals 14 HP. Feline Recovery Wrap heals 10 HP and cures Poison and Wither.
