Combat

Stalk and pounce

A cat creeps up crouched on an enemy that hasn't noticed it, then pounces as its opening move. Pounce Training adds reach and power.

Dodge and retreat

Cats dodge arrows and creeper blasts, and fall back to you when badly hurt (not in Aggressive mode).

Claw combo

Hits chain into a 4-step combo: normal, +15%, +30% with Bleed, then a +60% finisher with knockback. Hits 0.4 to 0.9 s apart are perfect: +25% damage and 10 Energy back.

Energy

Abilities, dodges and pounces cost Energy. Max Energy is 100, +2 per level (up to +100), plus talents and role. It refills 2/s in a fight, 4/s out of combat and 6/s while resting.
